About the Journal

About IJRPO

International Journal Research Paper Organization (IJRPO) is an international, open-access, peer-reviewed journal published online at ijrpo.in. It was started in 2026 to give students, early-career researchers and professionals a dependable place to publish original work.

All papers are free to read. Authors keep copyright, and published work is shared under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

Aims

  • Publish original, high-quality research that adds to knowledge in its field.
  • Give students and young researchers a fair and quick route to publication.
  • Keep every published paper free to read, cite and build on.
  • Uphold honest research practice through plagiarism checks and peer review.

Scope

IJRPO is multidisciplinary. We welcome research papers, review papers, case studies and survey papers in areas including, but not limited to:

Engineering & Technology

Computer science, AI & machine learning, data science, IT, electronics, electrical, mechanical, civil and chemical engineering.

Applied & Pure Sciences

Physics, chemistry, mathematics, biotechnology, environmental science, agriculture.

Management & Commerce

Business management, finance, marketing, HR, economics, entrepreneurship.

Humanities & Social Sciences

Education, psychology, law, sociology, literature, media and public policy.
