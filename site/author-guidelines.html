Author Guidelines

Before you submit

  • The paper must be original and not published or under review anywhere else.
  • Similarity (plagiarism) must be below 15% excluding references.
  • All authors must have agreed to the submission and the author order.
  • The paper must be written in clear English and follow the IJRPO template.

Paper format

File typeMS Word (.doc / .docx) for review; PDF is created by the journal
Page sizeA4, two-column layout, margins 0.75 inch
FontTimes New Roman — title 18 pt bold, headings 11 pt bold, body 10 pt
Length6 to 15 pages including figures and references
Title pageTitle, author names, department, institution, city, country, email of corresponding author
Abstract150–250 words, followed by 4–6 keywords
SectionsIntroduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results & Discussion, Conclusion, References
Figures & tablesNumbered, with captions, and cited in the text
ReferencesIEEE style, numbered in order of citation, e.g. [1], [2]

Review and publication process

  1. Submission — author submits the manuscript and receives a paper ID.
  2. Initial check — the editor checks scope, format and similarity (1–2 days).
  3. Peer review — double-blind review by at least two reviewers (5–7 days).
  4. Decision — accept, minor revision, major revision or reject, sent by email.
  5. Final submission — revised paper, signed copyright form and publication fee.
  6. Publication — paper published online in the next issue; e-certificates issued to all authors.

Documents for accepted papers

  • Final camera-ready paper in the IJRPO template
  • Signed copyright / licence agreement form [link to form]
  • Proof of payment of the publication fee
