Welcome to IJRPO

International Journal Research Paper Organization (IJRPO) is an open-access, double-blind peer-reviewed, monthly journal. It publishes original research papers, review articles and case studies in engineering, technology, computer science, applied sciences, management and humanities.

The journal is meant for students, research scholars, faculty and working professionals who want their work reviewed fairly, published quickly and read freely around the world.

Why publish with IJRPO

1

Peer review

Every manuscript is checked for plagiarism and reviewed by subject experts before it is accepted.

2

Fast decisions

First review decision usually within 7–10 days; accepted papers go online in the next issue.

3

Open access

All papers are free to read and download, published under CC BY 4.0.

4

Certificates

Every author on a published paper receives an e-certificate of publication.

5

Permanent record

Each paper gets its own page, a paper ID and a ready-to-use citation.

6

Many fields

Multidisciplinary scope, from AI and data science to commerce and social sciences.

How publishing works

  1. Submit your manuscript in the IJRPO template through the submission page or by email.
  2. Review: plagiarism check and peer review; you receive a decision by email.
  3. Acceptance: send the final paper and copyright form, and pay the publication fee.
  4. Publication: your paper goes online with its own page, and you receive your certificate.
