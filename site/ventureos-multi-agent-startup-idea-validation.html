VentureOS: A Retrieval-Grounded Multi-Agent System for Stage-Aware Startup Idea Validation

Yash Sharma, Ritesh Chandel
Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Dr. Akhilesh Das Gupta Institute of Professional Studies (Affiliated to Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University), Delhi, India

Read Full Paper (PDF) ⬇ Download PDF

Published inVolume 1, Issue 1 (November 2026)
Pages5–7
Published on25 November 2026
Paper IDIJRPO-V1I1-002
LicenceCC BY 4.0 (open access)

Abstract

Early-stage founders, particularly students and first-time builders, often lack access to analysts who can validate an idea against real market, financial and regulatory evidence. Large language models (LLMs) can draft such analyses, but a single prompt tends to produce fluent yet ungrounded output that does not acknowledge the limits of what it knows. This paper presents VentureOS, a multi-agent system that decomposes startup validation into twelve specialised agents orchestrated as a directed acyclic graph (DAG). A Research Agent retrieves live web evidence through a search API and injects it as shared context into downstream Market, Feasibility and Risk agents, following the retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) paradigm; a Synthesizer then fuses their outputs into an overall venture score. Limits are made explicit through a ten-factor weighted risk model with three actionable bands, geography-calibrated financial estimates, and stage-aware guardrails that change the advice for a student versus a funded startup. Results stream to a Next.js client over Server-Sent Events (SSE), with session state in Redis and history in MongoDB.
